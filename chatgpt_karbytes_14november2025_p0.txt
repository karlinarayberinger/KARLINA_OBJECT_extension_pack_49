Black metal and death metal are closely related extreme-metal genres, but they differ in **sound**, **vocals**, **themes**, and **aesthetic philosophy**. Here is a clear breakdown:

---

# **1. Overall Sound and Production**

### **Black Metal**

* **Thin, treble-heavy guitars** (a “buzzing” or “icy” tone).
* **Shrill, high-pitched tremolo picking** as the dominant riffing style.
* **Raw or lo-fi production** is often intentional — meant to sound cold, distant, or atmospheric.
* Often incorporates **ambient**, **folk**, or **symphonic** elements.

### **Death Metal**

* **Thick, bass-heavy guitars** with a powerful, punchy tone.
* **Complex, low-end riffing** with palm-muted chugs and fast technical passages.
* Generally **cleaner, more polished production** (even early bands tended to sound heavier and fuller).
* Often very **technical**, with frequent tempo and time-signature changes.

---

# **2. Vocals**

### **Black Metal**

* High-pitched **shrieks**, screams, and raspy vocals.
* Designed to sound like “agonized wailing” or “inhuman” and atmospheric.

### **Death Metal**

* Low-pitched **growls**, grunts, and guttural vocals.
* More percussive and physical, designed to add weight to the music.

---

# **3. Drumming and Rhythm**

### **Black Metal**

* Often uses **blast beats** but in a more continuous, “wall of sound” style.
* Rhythms tend to be **repetitive, trance-like, and hypnotic**.

### **Death Metal**

* Also uses blast beats, but with **greater rhythmic complexity**.
* Frequent **syncopation**, technical fills, stop-start patterns, and sudden shifts.

---

# **4. Themes and Aesthetics**

### **Black Metal**

* Extremely **atmospheric** and tied to mood.
* Common themes:

  * Anti-religion (especially anti-Christianity in early Scandinavian scenes)
  * Nature, winter, forests, cosmic emptiness
  * Mysticism, paganism, occultism
  * Nihilism, existentialism
* Visual aesthetic: **corpse paint**, spikes, medieval or pagan imagery.

### **Death Metal**

* Focuses on **brutality**, often with graphic themes:

  * Death, decay, violence
  * Horror, gore, anatomy
  * Morbidity and existential dread
  * Sometimes science fiction or the surreal
* Visual aesthetic: anatomical artwork, grotesque album covers, classic horror imagery.

---

# **5. Songwriting Approach**

### **Black Metal**

* Built around **atmosphere** and **feeling**.
* Long, repetitive structures.
* Often intentionally minimalist or trance-inducing.

### **Death Metal**

* Built around **technicality**, **precision**, and **musical complexity**.
* Riffs change frequently.
* More emphasis on musicianship and intricacy.

---

# **6. Cultural Origins**

### **Black Metal**

* Strong roots in **Norway** (Mayhem, Darkthrone, Emperor, Burzum).
* The second wave (early 1990s) defined the genre’s modern form.

### **Death Metal**

* Emerged mainly in **Florida** (Death, Morbid Angel, Obituary) and **New York**, plus early UK roots (Bolt Thrower, Carcass).
* Earlier bands like Possessed and Death formed the bridge from thrash.

---

# **7. Atmosphere: The Core Difference**

If you had to summarize in one sentence:

### **Death metal is focused on heaviness and technical brutality.

Black metal is focused on atmosphere, bleakness, and emotional evocation.**
